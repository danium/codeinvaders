# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for contributors of
all backgrounds and experience levels. Be constructive, assume good intent,
criticize ideas rather than people, respect privacy, and make technical
decisions from reproducible evidence.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, publishing private agent or user
data, and sustained disruption. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject
contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Do
not include sensitive agent data. Reports are handled as confidentially as
practical, with conflicts of interest disclosed and avoided.
